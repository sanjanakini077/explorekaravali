COASTAL KARNATAKA

Mangalore

Temples, Beaches & Coastal Heritage

Mangalore is a vibrant port city where spirituality, history, nature and coastal life come together.

TEMPLE

Kudroli Gokarnath Temple

Dedicated to Gokarnanatheshwara, this iconic temple is known for its Dravidian-inspired architecture, colourful celebrations and magnificent Gopuram.

Kudroli, Mangaluru

TEMPLE

Kadri Manjunatha Temple

Located at the foot of Kadri Hills, this historic temple combines spiritual significance with beautiful surroundings and traditional architecture.

Kadri, Mangaluru

TEMPLE

Mangaladevi Temple

One of Mangalore's important spiritual landmarks, the temple is dedicated to Goddess Mangaladevi and is deeply connected with the city's identity.

Bolar, Mangaluru

HERITAGE

St. Aloysius Chapel

Famous for its beautiful interiors, paintings and architectural character, this historic chapel is one of Mangalore's most distinctive landmarks.

Kodialbail, Mangaluru

HISTORY

Sultan Battery

A historic watchtower overlooking the Gurupura River, Sultan Battery offers visitors a glimpse into the military history of the region.

Urwa, Mangaluru

NATURE

Kadri Hill Park

A peaceful green space surrounded by the hills of Mangalore, perfect for relaxing away from the busy city.

Kadri, Mangaluru

The Spirit of Mangalore

From Yakshagana and temple festivals to coastal food and traditional communities, Mangalore's culture is deeply connected to its land and sea.
