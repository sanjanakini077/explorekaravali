DISCOVER MANGALURU

A City Shaped By The Sea

Mangaluru is a coastal city known for its port, beaches, religious landmarks, multicultural heritage and distinctive cuisine. Its cultural character reflects the many communities that have shaped life along the coast.

01

Temples

Kudroli Gokarnanatheshwara, Kadri Manjunatha and Mangaladevi are among the city's important religious landmarks.

02

Beaches

Panambur, Tannirbhavi and Surathkal offer beautiful stretches of the Arabian Sea.

03

Heritage

Sultan Battery, St. Aloysius Chapel and the old port reveal different layers of Mangaluru's history.

04

Food

Neer dosa, kori rotti, seafood, goli baje and Mangalore buns are part of the city's celebrated food culture.

PLACES TO VISIT

Mangaluru Highlights

TEMPLE

Kudroli Gokarnath Temple

A prominent temple dedicated to Gokarnanatheshwara, known for its striking architecture and annual celebrations.

TEMPLE

Kadri Manjunatha Temple

A historic temple located at the base of Kadri Hills, surrounded by a peaceful landscape.

TEMPLE

Mangaladevi Temple

A historic temple dedicated to Goddess Mangaladevi and an important spiritual landmark of the city.

HERITAGE

St. Aloysius Chapel

Known for its beautiful interior paintings, frescoes and distinctive architectural character.

HISTORY

Sultan Battery

A historic watchtower overlooking the waters near Mangaluru, associated with the city's maritime past.

NATURE

Kadri Hill Park

A green urban retreat with gardens, trees and spaces for relaxing away from the city's bustle.
