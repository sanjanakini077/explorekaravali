LIVING HERITAGE

The spirit of Karavali

01

Yakshagana

A spectacular traditional theatre form combining dance, music, elaborate costumes, storytelling and mythology. Yakshagana remains one of the most recognisable cultural traditions of coastal Karnataka.

02

Kambala

Kambala is the traditional buffalo race associated with the paddy fields of coastal Karnataka. It is deeply connected with rural life and community celebrations.

03

Bhoota Kola

A ritual performance tradition of Tulunadu involving music, dance, elaborate costumes and spiritual storytelling, forming an important part of local heritage.

04

Temple Traditions

From the Udupi Sri Krishna Temple to the many ancient shrines of Mangalore, temples remain important centres of devotion, art, music and community life.

05

Coastal Festivals

Festivals across the region bring together food, music, rituals and generations of families, preserving the distinctive identity of the coast.

06

Traditional Cuisine

Rice, coconut, seafood, spices and local ingredients form the foundation of a culinary tradition that reflects the geography and history of Karavali.
