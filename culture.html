COASTAL TRADITIONS

More Than History

The culture of Karavali continues to live through festivals, rituals, theatre, agriculture and community celebrations. Many traditions are deeply connected to the landscape and social life of Tulunadu and neighbouring coastal regions.

01
🎭

Yakshagana

A vibrant traditional dance-drama combining music, storytelling, elaborate costumes, dialogue and dramatic performance.

THEATRE · MUSIC · DANCE
02
🐃

Kambala

A traditional buffalo race connected with the agricultural landscape and heritage of Tulunadu.

AGRICULTURE · SPORT · TRADITION
03
🔥

Bhoota Kola

A sacred ritual tradition involving invocation, music, dance and community participation.

RITUAL · FAITH · COMMUNITY
04
🐅

Huli Vesha

A colourful tiger dance tradition seen during festivals and celebrations in coastal towns.

DANCE · FESTIVAL · CELEBRATION
05
🐍

Naga Aradhane

Traditional serpent worship practices deeply connected with the sacred landscape of Tulunadu.

FAITH · RITUAL · NATURE
06
🛕

Paryaya

A major Udupi tradition marking the ceremonial transfer of Sri Krishna Matha's management among the Ashta Mathas.

UDUPI · TEMPLE · TRADITION
07
🥥

Vitla Pindi

A lively Krishna Janmashtami celebration in Udupi associated with breaking decorated pots.

FESTIVAL · KRISHNA · COMMUNITY

Coastal Agriculture

Paddy fields, coconut palms and seasonal farming traditions remain an important part of the coastal landscape.
