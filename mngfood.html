Mangalore's Specialty

Gujje Podi

Also known as Kadgi Podi, Gujje Podi is a traditional coastal snack made from tender jackfruit, coated with a spiced batter and deep fried until crisp.

Hotel Woodlands

Charmuri

Charmuri is a popular Mangalorean street snack prepared with puffed rice, onion, carrot, cucumber, coconut, spices and fresh herbs.

Raja's Charmuri Stall

Golibaje

Golibaje, also called Mangalore Bajji, is a soft and fluffy deep-fried snack made using flour, curd, spices and herbs. It is best enjoyed hot with coconut chutney.

Hotel Woodlands

Kori Rotti

Kori Rotti is one of the most iconic dishes of coastal Karnataka. Crispy rice wafers are served with a rich, spicy chicken curry prepared with coconut and traditional Mangalorean spices.

Shetty's Kori Rotti

Neer Dosa with Chicken Sukka

Neer dosa is a thin and soft rice crepe traditionally paired with Chicken Sukka, a dry chicken preparation cooked with roasted coconut, spices and curry leaves.

Kadri Cafe

Pathrode

Pathrode is a traditional coastal delicacy made by layering colocasia leaves with a spiced rice, coconut and lentil mixture before steaming and slicing it into pieces.

Pallkhi Restaurant

Anjal Fry

Anjal Fry is a famous coastal seafood dish prepared by marinating kingfish with a spicy red masala and shallow frying it until crisp on the outside.

Machali GiriManja's

Mangalore Buns

Mangalore Buns are soft, fluffy and slightly sweet fried breads made with ripe bananas, flour, sugar and a touch of cumin.

Sagar Ratna

Kori Sukka with Rotti

Kori Sukka is a traditional Mangalorean dry chicken preparation made with roasted coconut, spices and curry leaves. It pairs beautifully with rice rotti, neer dosa or steamed rice.

Kori Pundi

Kori Pundi combines soft steamed rice dumplings with a flavourful chicken preparation. Pundi is a traditional coastal rice dish enjoyed with spicy curries.

Kadle Bajil

Kadle Bajil is a traditional Mangalorean snack made with puffed rice and roasted chickpeas, seasoned with coconut, spices, mustard seeds and curry leaves.

Bangude Fish Curry

Bangude Fish Curry is a classic Mangalorean coastal curry prepared with fresh mackerel, coconut, red chillies and tamarind. It is traditionally served with steamed rice or neer dosa.

Mangalore Fish Meal

A traditional Mangalorean fish meal brings together steamed rice, coastal fish curry, fried fish and delicious accompaniments, often served on a banana leaf.

Mangalore Style Fish Tawa Fry

Fresh coastal fish is marinated with a spicy red masala and cooked on a hot tawa until the exterior becomes crisp while the fish remains tender.

Clams Sukka

Clams Sukka is a traditional coastal seafood preparation made with fresh clams, grated coconut, roasted spices and curry leaves.
