Kori Sukka with Rotti

Kori Sukka is a traditional Mangalorean dry chicken preparation made with roasted coconut, spices and curry leaves. It is commonly enjoyed with rice rotti, neer dosa or rice.

Kori Pundi

Kori Pundi combines soft steamed rice dumplings with a flavourful chicken preparation. Pundi is a traditional coastal rice dish commonly paired with spicy curries.

Kadle Bajil

Kadle Bajil is a traditional Mangalorean snack made with puffed rice and roasted chickpeas, seasoned with coconut, spices and tempering.

Bangude Fish Curry

Bangude Fish Curry is a classic coastal curry made with mackerel and a spicy, tangy masala featuring coconut, chilli and tamarind. It is traditionally enjoyed with rice or neer dosa.

Mangalore Fish Meal

A traditional Mangalorean fish meal brings together steamed rice, coastal fish curry, fried fish and accompaniments for an authentic taste of the region.

Mangalore Style Fish Tawa Fry

Fresh coastal fish is marinated with a spicy red masala and cooked on a hot tawa until crisp outside while remaining tender inside.

Clams Sukka

Clams Sukka is a traditional coastal seafood preparation made with fresh clams, grated coconut, roasted spices and curry leaves. It is a popular dish in Mangalorean coastal cuisine.

THE COASTAL PLATE

Rice. Coconut. Spice. Sea.

Mangaluru's cuisine reflects its coastal landscape and diverse communities. Seafood and coconut-based preparations sit alongside vegetarian dishes, snacks and traditional rice preparations.
