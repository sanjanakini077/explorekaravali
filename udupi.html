DISCOVER UDUPI

A Coast With A Soul

Udupi is known for its spiritual heritage, distinctive vegetarian cuisine, beaches, islands and cultural traditions. The city and its surrounding region offer a unique combination of devotion, nature and coastal life.

01

Krishna Matha

One of South India's important pilgrimage centres and the heart of Udupi's spiritual identity.

02

Malpe

A popular coastal destination known for its beach, sea views and access to St. Mary's Island.

03

Kapu

A scenic beach famous for its lighthouse and panoramic Arabian Sea views.

04

Manipal

A university town known for education, modern culture and beautiful surrounding landscapes.

PLACES TO VISIT

Udupi Highlights

SPIRITUALITY

Udupi Sri Krishna Matha

The famous Krishna temple is associated with Madhvacharya and is known for the Kanakana Kindi window through which devotees traditionally view the deity.

BEACH

Malpe Beach

A beautiful coastal destination with golden sands, sea walks and access to nearby island experiences.

ISLAND

St. Mary's Island

Known for its distinctive rock formations, white sandy shores and scenic Arabian Sea setting.

COAST

Kapu Beach

A peaceful coastal destination where the historic lighthouse overlooks the Arabian Sea.

MODERN UDUPI

Manipal

A lively university town combining education, modern culture and the natural beauty of coastal Karnataka.

ADVENTURE

Kemmannu Hanging Bridge

A scenic bridge across the Swarna River surrounded by greenery and opportunities for outdoor experiences.
