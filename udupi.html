Temples, Islands & Serene Beaches

Udupi blends spiritual heritage, beautiful coastlines, traditional cuisine and a relaxed coastal atmosphere.

Udupi Sri Krishna Matha

One of South India's most important pilgrimage centres, the temple is famous for its unique worship traditions and the Navagraha Kitiki.

Udupi

BEACH

Malpe Beach

A beautiful coastal destination known for its golden sands, Arabian Sea views, water activities and relaxed atmosphere.

Malpe, Udupi

ISLAND

St. Mary's Island

Famous for its distinctive basalt rock formations and beautiful coastline, the island is one of Udupi's most memorable attractions.

Malpe, Udupi

A peaceful coastal escape featuring a historic lighthouse and spectacular views across the Arabian Sea.

Kapu, Udupi

Manipal

A lively university town known for education, modern development, green surroundings and a diverse student community.

Manipal, Udupi

Kemmannu Hanging Bridge

A scenic attraction over the Swarna River surrounded by greenery, offering a peaceful experience close to nature.

Kemmannu, Udupi
