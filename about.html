WHY EXPLORE KARAVALI?

Beyond The Tourist Map

Explore Karavali was created to showcase the diverse character of coastal Karnataka through destinations, food, culture and traditions.

Instead of looking at Mangaluru and Udupi only as tourist destinations, we wanted to explore the stories behind them — the festivals, temples, food, landscapes and communities that make the region unique.

From the Arabian Sea to the Western Ghats, Karavali is a place where nature and culture exist side by side.
