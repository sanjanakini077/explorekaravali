OUR PURPOSE

Celebrating the
Karavali coast

Explore Karavali was created to showcase the beauty and identity of coastal Karnataka. The region stretching across Mangalore, Udupi and surrounding areas is home to a remarkable combination of beaches, temples, forests, rivers, traditional art, festivals and food.

Our goal is to bring these experiences together in one place and make it easier for travellers to discover the destinations, traditions and flavours that make Karavali unique.
