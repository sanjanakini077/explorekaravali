About Explore Karavali

WELCOME

Discover the Soul of Coastal Karnataka

Explore Karavali is a digital travel experience created to showcase the beauty, culture, traditions and cuisine of coastal Karnataka.

The Karavali region is known for its breathtaking coastline, lush landscapes, ancient temples, historic landmarks and diverse culinary traditions.

Our goal is to bring these experiences together in one place and help travellers discover Mangalore, Udupi and the unique stories behind them.

01

Discover Karnataka

Explore beaches, temples, heritage sites, natural landscapes and hidden attractions across the coastal region.

02

Experience Culture

Learn about Yakshagana, traditional celebrations, temple customs, local communities and the cultural identity of Karavali.

03

Taste the Coast

Discover famous coastal dishes including Neer Dosa, Kori Rotti, Mangalore Buns, Pathrode, Udupi cuisine and more.

“Every journey through Karavali is a story waiting to be discovered.”
